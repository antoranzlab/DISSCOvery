# AlignQC Model Licence

The AlignQC model weights (`classification_network.h5`) are **not** covered by the
code licence and are **not** stored in this repository. They are distributed
separately under **noncommercial terms**.

Important provenance note: AlignQC is fine-tuned (transfer learning) from
**KimiaNet** (Riasatian et al., 2021), which is itself trained on **TCGA** data
and released for research / noncommercial use. Any downstream use of AlignQC —
including commercial use — is therefore subject to the upstream KimiaNet and TCGA
terms in addition to these.

Before relying on any commercial use of the model, confirm the position with
KU Leuven LRD (tech transfer). Nothing here is legal advice.
